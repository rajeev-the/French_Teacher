A Personal Approach to Learning

“Learning should never feel like a race to memorise steps. It should be a quiet, steady journey where a student comes to trust their own mind.”

SA
Shikha Arya Mathematics & French Specialist • 10 Years Teaching

Over the past ten years of teaching Mathematics and French, I have worked with a diverse spectrum of students—each with distinct learning habits, aspirations, and moments of hesitation. I believe deeply that meaningful academic progress does not come from memorising disconnected formulas or learning phrases by rote. Real mastery takes root when a student understands the fundamental logic beneath every principle.

Whether we are working through intricate algebraic equations or developing nuanced French grammar and pronunciation, my teaching is grounded in five core commitments:

  • Understanding Concepts Rather than Memorising Answers: Ensuring students know why a mathematical formula holds true, or how a French sentence structure conveys subtle meaning.
  • Developing Confidence Through Consistent Practice: Providing step-by-step guidance so students can transition from hesitant uncertainty to independent problem-solving.
  • Adapting Explanations to Individual Learning Needs: Recognising that every learner processes information differently, and adjusting analogies, pace, and exercises accordingly.
  • Encouraging Curiosity and Independent Thinking: Creating a space where questions are welcomed and mistakes are treated as natural milestones in learning.
  • Creating a Supportive, Respectful Environment: Maintaining patience and encouragement so students feel secure taking on challenging concepts.

Guiding Principles

Learning Should Build Understanding and Confidence

A decade in education reinforces three central tenets that shape every lesson, explanation, and exercise.

01

Clarity Before Complexity

Difficult concepts become manageable when broken into clear, logical steps. Rather than rushing ahead into complex problems or advanced texts, we first build an unshakeable foundation so students never feel overwhelmed.

02

Practice With Purpose

Repetition alone is not enough; practice must be deliberate and thoughtful. We use carefully chosen exercises, constructive feedback, and regular revision to reinforce concepts until they become natural instincts.

03

Teaching That Adapts

No two students learn at the identical rhythm. As an educator with 10 years of experience, I adapt explanations, analogies, and pacing to match each student's unique learning style, strengths, and areas requiring patience.

Lesson Structure

Thoughtful Lessons, Meaningful Progress

What students and parents can expect from our regular sessions:

Phase 1

Discussion & Diagnostics

A brief check-in to review prior topics, understand immediate homework or exam goals, and gauge current comprehension.

Phase 2

Tailored Explanations

Clear, patient walkthroughs of new or challenging topics using accessible analogies suited to the student's level.

Phase 3

Guided & Solo Practice

Working through initial examples collaboratively before the student tackles practice problems independently.

Phase 4

Targeted Feedback

Immediate, supportive error analysis to ensure small misunderstandings are resolved before they cause confusion.

Phase 5

Revision & Reinforcement

Consolidating key takeaways and setting structured practice tasks to cement retention between sessions.

* Note: The exact structure of each session is flexible and adapted to individual learning needs, school curricula, and upcoming academic deadlines.
